Understanding Dividend Dates

📋 Declaration Date

The board officially announces the dividend amount per share. Share price usually rises after this announcement.

🔴 Ex-Dividend Date

You MUST own the shares BEFORE this date to qualify for the dividend. Buy on or after this date and you miss the payment.

📸 Record Date

The company checks its shareholder register. If your name is there, you receive the dividend.

💰 Payment Date

The actual date your dividend is credited to your brokerage account. Usually 2–4 weeks after the record date.
