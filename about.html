The Story That Started Everything
₦6,000,000+
Lost to a wrong investment in 2023

In 2023, I lost over six million naira. Not to fraud. Not to a scam. To a legitimate investment that I simply did not understand well enough before I put my money in. No one explained the risks clearly. No one walked me through the fundamentals. I made a decision with incomplete information — and I paid for it with years of savings. That loss changed everything.

Why I Built Rise With Stocks

After losing that money, I made a decision. I was not going to let the same thing happen to me again — and I was not going to let it happen to people around me if I could help it.

I went back to studying. I deepened my understanding of capital markets, investment structures, and financial instruments. I earned my ACIB qualification with a distinction in Corporate Financial Services. I read everything I could about how the Nigerian Exchange works, how IPOs are structured, how dividends are paid, and how retail investors like us actually make — or lose — money.

And then the Dangote Refinery IPO was announced.

I saw the same pattern forming that had burned me in 2023. Nigerians were excited. People were talking. But almost nobody had clear, accurate, plain-English information about what this IPO actually was, how to participate, what a CHN number is, what a CSCS account does, whether diaspora Nigerians could join, whether people in South Africa or Kenya could invest. The hype was everywhere. The education was nowhere.

“I lost ₦6 million because I didn’t have the right information at the right time. I built Rise With Stocks so that no one else has to lose theirs for the same reason.”

So I built Rise With Stocks. A platform with one mission: give every Nigerian and African investor the financial education they deserve, before they commit their money — not after.
