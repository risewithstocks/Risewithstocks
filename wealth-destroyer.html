Why This Calculator Matters for Nigerian Investors

📈 The Inflation Tax

Inflation silently erodes your purchasing power every single day. ₦1 million today buys what ₦862,000 would buy next year at 15.91% inflation.

🤝 The Savings Account Trap

Most Nigerian savings accounts pay 4–12%. With inflation at 15.91%, you are losing real wealth even while your balance appears to grow.

💵 Real Return Formula

Real return = ((1 + investment rate) / (1 + inflation rate)) − 1. Not simply return minus inflation — that understates the real damage.
