🇳🇬 Nigeria — Primary Listing

Nigeria is where the IPO starts. The primary listing on the NGX is the main event — and Nigerian retail investors have the most direct access.

4 Officially Appointed Brokers

Zedcrest Securities · Stanbic IBTC · Vetiva Securities · FirstCap Securities. Only these 4 submit applications directly to the issuing house.

How to Invest from Nigeria

1

Open a Brokerage Account

Download your chosen broker's app. Upload BVN, NIN, and passport photo. Account opens within 1–2 days.

2

Get Your CHN & CSCS Number

Your broker will assign your Clearing House Number (CHN) and CSCS account. These are required for your application to be valid.

3

Fund Your Wallet

Transfer money into your brokerage wallet before the subscription window opens. The funds must be there before you apply.

4

Apply During the Window

When the window opens in September, submit your application through your broker's app. Apply early.

5

Receive Allotment

Shares are credited to your CSCS account. Unallotted funds are refunded within 4–8 weeks.

Africa's richest country and most sophisticated stock market. The JSE secondary listing opens the Dangote IPO to South African investors and institutions.

Why South Africa?

The JSE is Africa's largest stock exchange by market cap. South African investors manage the continent's biggest pension funds. Institutional interest in the Dangote IPO from SA is enormous.

1

Open a JSE-Registered Broker Account

Use any FSP (Financial Services Provider) licensed by the FSCA. Major JSE brokers include EasyEquities, Sanlam, and PSG Securities.

2

Watch for the JSE Listing Announcement

The JSE secondary listing terms will be announced alongside the NGX primary listing. Pricing will be in Rand at the equivalent rate.

3

Subscribe Through Your SA Broker

Applications can be submitted through your JSE broker's platform during the same subscription window.
