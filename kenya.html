Common questions

What Kenyan investors are asking

Can I participate as a Kenyan investor?

Yes — and you do not need a Nigerian bank account, BVN, or CSCS account. Kenyan investors can subscribe RIGHT NOW through Daba Finance (dabafinance.com/en/dangote-ipo) or MyStocks Africa (mystocks.africa/dangote-ipo). Both platforms use an omnibus model with local KYC.

What is the minimum I can invest?

The minimum subscription is 10 shares at ₦525 per share — approximately KSh 650 at current exchange rates. Subscribe through Daba Finance or MyStocks Africa in KSh with no Nigerian account needed. In practice, most investors subscribe for significantly more to increase their allocation chances in an oversubscribed offering.

Will I receive dividends in Kenyan Shillings or US Dollars?

This depends on your investment structure. Investors who hold a Certificate of Capital Importation (CCI) — confirming funds entered through official forex channels — may qualify for dollar dividends. Without a CCI, dividends are paid in naira at prevailing exchange rates. Your guide covers this in full.

Is this IPO open to everyone or just institutions?

Both. The prospectus has a retail investor tranche specifically for individual investors like you. Retail investors may also receive up to 2 free bonus shares for maintaining their minimum shareholding for the applicable holding period.

What happens if the IPO is oversubscribed?

You may receive fewer shares than you applied for. Any excess funds are refunded promptly to your broker account. The issuer may absorb up to 30% additional shares with SEC approval to accommodate excess demand.
